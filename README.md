## Future Interns – Data Science & Analytics Internship

### Task 2: Customer Churn Analysis

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the **Future Interns Data Science & Analytics Internship – Task 2**.

The objective of this project is to analyze customer churn data and understand customer retention patterns, churn rate, contract types, payment methods, internet services, and customer tenure.

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** was created to provide a clear and visual overview of customer churn and identify areas where customer retention can be improved.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze the overall customer churn rate.
- Identify the total number of customers and churned customers.
- Understand churn distribution.
- Analyze churn across different contract types.
- Compare churn by payment methods.
- Analyze churn based on internet service.
- Understand the relationship between customer tenure and churn.
- Create an interactive and client-ready Power BI dashboard.

## 🔍 Key Insights

- The dataset contains approximately **7.043K customers**.
- **1.869K customers** are classified as churned.
- The overall customer churn rate is approximately **26.54%**.
- Month-to-month contracts show a substantially larger number of churned customers compared with longer-term contracts.
- Electronic check is the payment method associated with the largest number of churned customers in the dashboard.
- Fiber optic customers represent the largest churn count among the displayed internet service categories.
- The tenure analysis shows that churn is concentrated more heavily among customers with shorter tenure.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, businesses can consider:

- Developing retention strategies for **month-to-month customers**.
- Identifying early-stage customers who may be at higher risk of churn.
- Investigating customer experience among **fiber optic users**.
- Reviewing payment experience for customers using **electronic checks**.
- Offering suitable incentives for customers considering longer-term contracts.
- Using customer churn analysis to develop targeted retention campaigns.
